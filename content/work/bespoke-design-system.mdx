Northwind Health had accumulated four separate design languages over five years of rapid acquisition. Each platform team maintained their own component library, their own token vocabulary, and their own Figma workspace — with no shared governance model to tie any of it together.

Helix-33 was the response: a single source of truth for all UI decisions across web, iOS, and Android.
