Two apps. Two marketing sites. Four surfaces, each shipping their own buttons, cards, and forms — with one person responsible for making sure they didn't have to.

Hitch closed that gap by designing and building the system solo: every component specified in Figma and shipped in Vue by the same hands, so nothing was lost in translation between the two disciplines.
