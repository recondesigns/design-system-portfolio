AI product teams are shipping chat interfaces, streaming responses, and agent-driven UI faster than most component libraries can support them — and accessibility is usually the first casualty.

atomly-ai closes that gap: an open-source system where every atom and molecule ships with focus management, live-region support, and keyboard interaction built in, not bolted on after launch.
