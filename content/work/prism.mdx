Five product lines. Five brand identities. One engineering team trying to maintain five separate component libraries without losing their minds.

Prism solved this by inverting the typical relationship between tokens and components. Instead of components owning their visual decisions, every value — color, spacing, radius, typography — was expressed as a semantic token that each brand could override independently.
