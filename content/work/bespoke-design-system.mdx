Six product surfaces. No shared component library. Every team maintaining their own button, their own modal, their own interpretation of what accessible meant in code.

Bespoke fixed that by building on React Aria from day one — 34 components, from single atoms to compound systems like Data Table and Dialog Modal, all accessible by default. Three teams have since adopted it across all six surfaces.
